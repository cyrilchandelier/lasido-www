Privacy Policy

Last updated — January 2025

Overview

LASIDO ("the App") is developed by Cyril Chandelier. This Privacy Policy describes how the App handles information. LASIDO does not collect any personal data. The App uses third-party services for anonymous usage analytics and crash reporting only.

Data collection

The App does not collect any personally identifiable information. No accounts, no names, no email addresses, no personal data of any kind. Your practice sessions, scores, and progress remain entirely on your device.

Analytics & crash reporting

The App uses third-party services to collect anonymous usage data and crash reports. This helps us understand how the App is used and to fix issues. The data collected is aggregated and cannot be used to identify individual users. These services may collect device information such as device model, operating system version, and app version. No personal information is included in these reports.

Data storage

All user-generated application data — including your settings and game progress — is stored locally on your device. Only anonymous analytics and crash data are transmitted to third-party services as described above.

Children's privacy

LASIDO is rated 4+ and is suitable for all ages. The App does not collect any personally identifiable information from any user, including children, and complies with children's privacy regulations including COPPA.

Changes to this policy

If this Privacy Policy is updated, the changes will be reflected on this page with a revised date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you may contact the developer via the App Store listing for LASIDO.
